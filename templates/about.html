About ResumePro

ResumePro is a free resume-builder web app that lets you create a polished, professional resume in minutes. Pick from 4 designed templates, fill in your details, adjust your photo, get AI-powered writing suggestions for your summary and job descriptions, and download a pixel-accurate PDF whenever you're ready.

Features
  • 4 resume templates: Modern, Professional, Creative, Executive
  • Pixel-accurate PDF download — matches the on-screen preview exactly
  • Crop / zoom / adjust your profile photo before uploading
  • AI Assistant (Gemini 2.5 Flash) for summaries, skills and job descriptions
  • One-click Print button
